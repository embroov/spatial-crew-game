=======================================================
 🎵 CUSTOM MUSIC DIRECTORY: public/music/
=======================================================

Place your MP3 song files inside this folder to play them in the DJ Room!

Supported filenames:
  - song1.mp3  (Local Song 1)
  - song2.mp3  (Local Song 2)
  - song3.mp3  (Local Song 3)
  - song4.mp3  (Local Song 4)
  - song5.mp3  (Local Song 5)

You can name your MP3 files as `song1.mp3`, `song2.mp3`, etc. and select them directly from the in-game DJ Console!
